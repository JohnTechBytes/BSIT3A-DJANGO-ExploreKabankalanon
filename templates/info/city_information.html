ABOUT THE CITY

About Kabankalan

A short overview adapted from Wikipedia, with links to original sources where available. Open a topic to learn more. The guide search above searches published guides, not these summaries.

City overview and government

Kabankalan is a component city in Negros Occidental, in the Negros Island Region of the Philippines. Republic Act No. 8297 established its city charter. The city has a mayor, vice mayor and city council; consult official sources for current officials.

Read the city charter

Attractions and celebrations

Wikipedia names Mag-aso Falls and Balicaocao Resort among local attractions. It also mentions Sinulog sa Kabankalan, Udyakan sa Kabankalan and the city’s Charter Anniversary. Check official announcements for current festival programs.

Read the article overview

History and cityhood

Wikipedia describes the name as connected to the locally named bangkal tree and recounts the area’s Spanish, American and wartime history. Its history section is marked as lacking sources, so those accounts need further verification. Wikipedia dates cityhood to August 2, 1997; the city charter is Republic Act No. 8297.

Read the history section

Geography

Kabankalan is in southern Negros. Wikipedia lists Himamaylan, Ilog, Mabinay and Bayawan among its neighboring areas and reports an area of 697.35 square kilometers.

Read about its geography

Climate

Wikipedia describes a Type III climate, with a drier period from December to May and a wetter period from June to November. These are broad patterns, not a forecast; check current weather before traveling.

Read the climate information

Barangays

The Philippine Statistics Authority lists 32 barangays in Kabankalan as of July 31, 2025. They include Barangays 1–9 in the poblacion and communities such as Oringao, Tampalon and Tapi.

See the PSA barangay list

Population and demographics

The Philippine Statistics Authority lists a population of 210,893 for Kabankalan in the 2024 census. This is a census count, not a live population figure.

See the PSA city and barangay figures

Languages

Wikipedia identifies Hiligaynon and Cebuano as major local languages and also mentions Tagalog, English and the Karol-an language.

Read about languages

Economy

The article includes historical poverty estimates from the Philippine Statistics Authority and describes the role of sugar and trade in the city’s development. Economic figures refer to particular years and can change.

See the economy section and its sources

Transportation and infrastructure

Wikipedia describes road connections between Kabankalan, Bacolod and Dumaguete via the Kabankalan–Mabinay route. Its statement about the city airport cites a 2014 report, so its current status should be checked separately. Confirm routes, travel times and services locally before a trip.

Read the transportation section

Notable people

The article lists people associated with Kabankalan from public service, religion, writing and sport, including Alex Lacson and Jonah Sabete. See Wikipedia for the full list and individual references.

See the notable people list
